WELCOME TO VANAMADEVI COMMON SERVICES CENTRE

The Common Services Centre (CSC) at Vanamadevi acts as a strategic access point for delivering essential government-to-citizen (G2C) and business-to-citizen (B2C) e-services directly to rural citizens.

Our center brings digital administrative assistance right to your doorstep, saving time and simplifying official submissions for government applications, certificates, and utility services.

OUR PURPOSE & AIM

Our mission is to bridge the digital gap in rural communities and make digital governance accessible, transparent, and hassle-free for every resident of Vanamadevi and surrounding villages.

  • Empowerment: Providing local citizens with direct online access to state and central government services (இ-சேவை).
  • Convenience: Eliminating long travel times by offering Aadhaar updates, revenue certificates, and banking support locally.
  • Transparency: Ensuring fair, secure, and fee-standardized public administrative assistance.
  • Financial Inclusion: Facilitating door-step banking services, pension program enrolments, and digital transactions.
