ABOUT VANAMADEVI CSC CENTER

Established in 2017

Our Journey Since 2017

Established in 2017, the Vanamadevi Common Services Centre (இ-சேவை மையம்) has served as a reliable rural e-Governance portal for residents across Vanamadevi Panchayat and surrounding regions in Cuddalore district.

Over years of continuous operation, our center has delivered public e-services, financial inclusion schemes, Aadhaar updates, and administrative grievance assistance directly to citizens at their doorstep.

Why Choose Vanamadevi CSC Center?

Our commitment is centered around rural digital empowerment, accuracy, and hassle-free service delivery:

  • Proven Experience: Serving local citizens reliably since 2017 with deep knowledge of state and central government portals.
  • Complete e-Services (இ-சேவை): Fast application processing for revenue certificates, community certificates, and welfare schemes.
  • Aadhaar & Digital Assistance: On-site demographic updates, UCL support, and identity documentation assistance.
  • Financial & Pension Inclusion: Direct enrolment support for PM-SYM, Atal Pension Yojana (APY), NPS, and Banking services.
  • Transparent Assistance: Guided assistance for filing grievances on CPGRAMS and official tracking.

Our Future Plans & Vision

We are continuously expanding our service offerings and infrastructure to better serve the rural community:

  • Enhanced Digital Skill Training: Launching localized online soft skills, computer literacy, and AI orientation programs for youth.
  • Expanded Banking Services: Increasing doorstep cash disbursement and micro-banking accessibility for senior citizens and farmers.
  • Farmer & Agricultural Support: Broadening enrolment drives for Crop Insurance (Rabi/Kharif) and PM-Kisan benefits.
  • Online Appointment & Queue System: Upgrading digital booking platforms to reduce wait times at the center.
